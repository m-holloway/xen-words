# Privacy Policy for Xen Words

**Last Updated:** November 12, 2025  
**Effective Date:** November 12, 2025

## Introduction

Xen Words is a speech-based sight words learning app designed for children ages 4-8. We take your child's privacy extremely seriously. This app is designed to be **100% offline** and collects **zero personal information**.

## Our Privacy Commitment

**We Do NOT:**
- ❌ Collect any personal information
- ❌ Record or store audio files
- ❌ Use any analytics or tracking
- ❌ Show any advertisements
- ❌ Share any data with third parties
- ❌ Require account creation
- ❌ Connect to the internet for any functionality

**We DO:**
- ✅ Process speech recognition entirely on your device
- ✅ Store app settings and progress locally on your device only
- ✅ Allow you to delete all data at any time through your device settings

## COPPA Compliance

This app is designed for children under 13 and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we collect **no personal information whatsoever**, no parental consent for data collection is required.

## Information We Do Not Collect

### Audio Data
- Speech recognition happens **entirely on-device** using offline models
- Audio is processed in real-time and **immediately discarded**
- No audio recordings are saved to storage
- No audio is ever transmitted over the internet

### Personal Information
We do not collect:
- Names (child name for rug personalization is stored locally only)
- Ages or birthdates
- Email addresses
- Photos or videos
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- IP addresses

### Usage Data
We do not collect:
- Analytics or usage statistics
- Session data
- Performance metrics
- Crash reports (in early access - may add privacy-friendly crash reporting with clear disclosure)

## Data Storage

The only data stored on your device:
1. **App Settings**: Display preferences, audio settings (stored locally)
2. **Progress Data**: Words completed, weeks unlocked (stored locally)
3. **Visual Settings**: Child name for rug display, camera settings (stored locally)

All data is stored using your device's standard app storage and is:
- Only accessible by this app
- Deleted when you uninstall the app
- Never backed up to cloud services without your device-level permissions
- Never transmitted over the internet

## Third-Party Services

We do **not** use any third-party services, including:
- Analytics platforms
- Advertising networks
- Cloud storage
- Social media integrations
- Payment processors (for future in-app purchases, handled entirely by Apple/Google)

## Parental Rights

As a parent or guardian, you can:
- Review all locally stored data through the app's settings
- Delete all app data by uninstalling the app
- Control app permissions through your device settings
- Contact us with any privacy questions

## Changes to Settings

Access to app settings and data viewing is protected by a parental gate to prevent children from accidentally changing settings or accessing progress data.

## Data Security

Since all processing happens on-device and we collect no data:
- There is no server to breach
- There is no database to hack
- Your child's information never leaves your device

## Future Updates

If we ever change our privacy practices (for example, adding optional cloud backup), we will:
1. Update this privacy policy
2. Notify users through an in-app message
3. Require explicit parental consent
4. Make the feature entirely optional
5. Maintain the fully offline mode as default

## Children's Privacy

This app is designed to be safe for children under 13. We:
- Do not collect any personal information from children
- Do not enable children to make their information publicly available
- Do not condition participation on providing more information than necessary
- Process all data locally on the device

## Your Consent

By using Xen Words, you consent to this privacy policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please uninstall the app.
