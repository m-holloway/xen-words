=== STORY TEXT (NARRATION ONLY) ===
On Christmas Eve, AZ and her little sister Adalyn lay tucked in their bunk beds, watching the snowflakes dance outside their window. The colored lights on their tree twinkled like tiny stars. AZ's mp3 player hummed a soft K-pop tune as she braided her golden hair. Eight-year-old Addy, her hazel eyes wide behind her glasses, sketched candy canes in her notebook.

Suddenly, the Christmas lights flickered. Not with light—with darkness. A shadow crept along the wire, swallowing each bulb's glow. Addy gasped. 'AZ, look!' The shadow moved like spilled ink, turning their merry tree dull and gray. From the corner, their fluffy white cat Mittens hissed, his whiskers twitching wildly.

Mittens leaped onto the windowsill and pawed at the glass. Outside, where their yard should be, a swirling portal of peppermint stripes spun in the air. 'We have to go,' AZ whispered, grabbing her sister's hand. Addy nodded, pulling on her glasses. Together, they stepped through the portal, Mittens bounding ahead.

They landed in Candy Cane Lane, but something was terribly wrong. The candy canes drooped like wilted flowers. The chocolate river had frozen solid. Sugar snowflakes fell as plain, white ash. A tiny elf rushed toward them, her face streaked with tears. 'The Giggle-Gobbler! He's stolen every laugh and smile!'

The elf led them to a palace made of gingerbread and gumdrops. On the throne sat the Candy Cane Queen, her short red hair glowing like warm fire against the gray world. Her crown sparkled with the letters 'TQ.' In her hand she held not a wand, but a sparkly tennis racquet sceptre. 'My darlings,' she said, her voice like their grandmother's hug, 'we need your help.'

The Queen's eyes, kind and bright, filled with tears. 'Grumblesnatch the Giggle-Gobbler was once a happy creature. But loneliness turned his heart sour. Now he's built a fortress of frowns and plans to steal Christmas itself.' She showed them a map drawn on fruit leather. 'Here, at the top of Mount Peppermint, he keeps the giggles locked in a box.'

AZ studied the map, her fingers tracing the route. 'Addy, look—first we cross the Sour Swamp, then climb the Gumdrop Cliffs.' Addy bounced on her toes, her energy buzzing despite the gloom. 'I can do it! I'm small but I'm fast!' Mittens meowed in agreement, his white fur puffing up with courage.

Their first challenge came quickly. The Sour Swamp bubbled with sticky, green goo that smelled like old socks. 'Hold your nose!' AZ laughed, though her laugh sounded thin without the world's giggles. She used her aerial silk skills, tying a rope of licorice to swing across. Addy followed, her small feet light on the stepping stones. Mittens simply leaped from log to log, his whiskers never drooping.

At the Gumdrop Cliffs, they found the candy rocks crumbling. 'We need to fly!' Addy cried. The Queen had given them two magic candy canes that hummed with sugar-spun magic. AZ climbed first, her tall frame reaching handholds easily. She belayed Addy up, calling encouragement. 'You're doing great, Addy! Just like we practiced on the jungle gym!'

Grumblesnatch's fortress loomed ahead—a tower of frowns carved from stale fruitcake. The sisters crept through a window of frozen molasses. Inside, they saw him: a round, furry creature with a long nose that wobbled like gelatin. His tiny eyes were red from crying. Around him, a box of captured giggles glowed faintly, trying to escape.

'STOP!' Grumblesnatch bellowed, his voice cracking. He trapped them in a cage made of grumpy sighs. Addy's hands clenched into fists. 'We should yell at him!' she whispered. But AZ remembered the Queen's words. She saw the tears on the creature's furry cheeks. 'Wait,' she said softly. 'Let's try something else.'

AZ reached into her pocket and pulled out a tiny craft she'd made—a small loom-woven bracelet of rainbow threads. 'We came to give you this,' she said gently. Addy, understanding, softened her voice. 'Why are you so sad?' Grumblesnatch's wobbly nose quivered. 'No one ever gave me a gift before,' he sniffled. 'Everyone just calls me grumpy.'

The cage of sighs began to dissolve. Addy reached through the bars and patted his furry hand. 'We can be friends,' she offered. 'Friends?' Grumblesnatch repeated, as if tasting a new word. His tiny eyes grew wider. The bracelet in his paw seemed to glow. Slowly, he unlocked the giggle-box. Laughter burst out like sunshine through clouds.

Giggles swirled around them, lifting their spirits. Grumblesnatch's own laugh started as a rumble, then grew into a booming chuckle. His fur seemed to brighten from gray to a warm brown. 'I'm sorry,' he said, his voice now soft. 'I didn't know kindness felt so... so...' 'So good?' AZ finished, smiling.

Back at the palace, the Candy Cane Queen prepared a grand celebration. Elves hung lights that sang K-pop carols. The chocolate river flowed again, bubbling with joy. Mittens batted at dancing sugarplums. Grumblesnatch, now part of the team, helped decorate with giant ornaments. His wobbly nose wiggled with happiness.

The royal reception began with fanfare from peppermint horns. The Queen, radiant in her shimmering gown, welcomed them onto a balcony. 'Today,' she announced, 'courage and kindness saved Christmas!' Grumblesnatch stood beside the sisters, wearing his rainbow bracelet. The crowd cheered. Fireworks of glittering sugar burst overhead.

Then came a hush. From every corner of the world, children had sent messages—drawings, letters, tiny crafts. The Queen held up a crystal that glowed with all their love. 'This is the Crystal of Kindness,' she said. 'Given to those who transform fear into friendship.' She placed it around AZ and Addy's necks, where it sparkled like a thousand Christmas lights.

Addy's eyes grew as wide as saucers behind her glasses. 'It's so beautiful!' she whispered. AZ felt tears prick her own blue eyes. The crystal hummed with warmth, and she could almost hear the voices of children laughing. Grumblesnatch bowed low. 'You taught me the best gift of all,' he said. 'Now I have friends.'

As the party danced on, the Queen guided them back to the portal. 'Christmas morning awaits,' she said, hugging them both. Mittens purred in Addy's arms. Grumblesnatch gave them each a candy cane that would let them return anytime. 'For when you need more adventure,' he said, his nose wobbling with a smile.

They tumbled back into their room just as the clock struck midnight. The tree glowed brighter than ever. Under it, two new presents appeared—one containing a tiny loom for AZ, and one with craft supplies for Addy. And on their nightstand sat the Crystal of Kindness, humming softly. Mittens curled up between them, his whiskers twitching in his sleep, dreaming of candy canes and forever friends.
