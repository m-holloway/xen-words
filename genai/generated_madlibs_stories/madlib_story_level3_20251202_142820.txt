Title: The Kite That Loved a Ribbon
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child helps a stuck kite with the help of a pet and a friendly animal, learning kindness and courage.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the child
  Child prompt: What is your name?
  Examples: Mia, Noah, Luna
- slot_toy (toy_object): Toy the child plays with
  Child prompt: What toy would you love to play with?
  Examples: kite, ball, doll
- slot_place (place_name): Place where the child plays
  Child prompt: Where do you like to play?
  Examples: park, garden, beach
- slot_pet (animal_pet): Pet that helps the child
  Child prompt: What pet would you like to have?
  Examples: bunny, dog, cat
- slot_snack (snack_food): Snack that makes the child brave
  Child prompt: What snack makes you feel brave?
  Examples: candy apple, banana, cookie
- slot_friend (animal_friend): Friendly animal that helps
  Child prompt: What friendly animal would help?
  Examples: turtle, bird, elephant
- slot_color (color): Color of the kite ribbon
  Child prompt: What color would you choose for the ribbon?
  Examples: bright blue, emerald green, crimson red

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: One sunny day, the kite got stuck high in a tree.
s3: [[slot_child]] looked up and felt a little sad.
s4: [[slot_pet]] hopped over and said, "I can help!"
s5: Together they tried to climb, but the branch was too high.
s6: [[slot_child]] thought about climbing alone, but then remembered the [[slot_snack]] on the bench.
s7: The snack was sweet, and it made [[slot_child]] feel brave.
s8: [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] sang a gentle song to the kite.
s9: The song made the kite wobble, and a friendly [[slot_friend]] fluttered down.
s10: The [[slot_friend]] lifted the kite down with its tiny feet.
s11: [[slot_child]] thanked the [[slot_friend]] and gave the kite a new [[slot_color]] ribbon.
s12: The kite flew happily, and [[slot_child]] felt warm inside.
