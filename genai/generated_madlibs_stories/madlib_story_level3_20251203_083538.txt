Title: The Secret Detour
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A bored ride becomes a magical adventure when a secret turn reveals a giant animal and a sparkling souvenir.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the child main character.
  Child prompt: What is a name you like for the kid in this story?
  Examples: Mia, Jamal, Sofia
- slot_vehicle (vehicle_type): The type of vehicle the child is riding in.
  Child prompt: What kind of vehicle is the child riding? (e.g., school bus, subway train, mom's van)
  Examples: school bus, subway train, mom's van
- slot_driver (character_role): Who is driving or steering the vehicle.
  Child prompt: Who is driving the vehicle? (e.g., driver with a purple hat, robot conductor, grandpa)
  Examples: driver with a purple hat, robot conductor, grandpa
- slot_animal (animal_name): A large animal that floats past the window.
  Child prompt: Name a big animal you like. (e.g., whale, giraffe, dragon)
  Examples: whale, giraffe, dragon
- slot_fantasy_sight (magical_entity): A fantastical thing seen outside the vehicle.
  Child prompt: What magical thing would you like to see outside? (e.g., flying whales, candy castles, dancing dinosaurs)
  Examples: flying whales, candy castles, dancing dinosaurs
- slot_souvenir (small_object): A small proof that the detour was real.
  Child prompt: What small souvenir would you keep? (e.g., glowing ticket, blue leaf, star-shaped stone)
  Examples: glowing ticket, blue leaf, star-shaped stone

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] stared out the window of the [[slot_vehicle]] and felt a little bored.
s2: The driver, [[slot_driver]], was humming a song while the road stretched gray and plain.
s3: Suddenly, the vehicle turned onto a street that wasn't on any map, and the sky turned pink.
s4: [[slot_child]] pressed their face to the glass and saw a giant [[slot_animal]] floating gracefully past the window.
s5: The animal waved, and tiny sparkles fell into the car, making the seats sparkle.
s6: Inside, the driver laughed and said, "We're on a secret adventure!"
s7: The vehicle zoomed through a field of [[slot_fantasy_sight]] for just a minute, and everything felt like a dream.
s8: Then the road became ordinary again, but the vehicle headed back to the usual route.
s9: When they arrived, [[slot_child]] held a [[slot_souvenir]] that glowed like a star, proof of the detour.
s10: Everyone in the vehicle cheered, and the trip felt wonderful and full of wonder.
