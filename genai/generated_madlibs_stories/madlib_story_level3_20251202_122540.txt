Title: Mia and the Lost Kite
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A gentle story about Mia, a small problem, and a happy ending.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): A child name
  Child prompt: What's your name?
  Examples: Mia, Liam, Emma
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child loves
  Child prompt: What is your favorite toy?
  Examples: ball, doll, car
- slot_color (color): A bright color
  Child prompt: Name a bright color you like.
  Examples: red, blue, green
- slot_animal (animal_pet): A friendly animal to help
  Child prompt: Name a friendly animal that could be a friend.
  Examples: dog, cat, rabbit
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack to share
  Child prompt: What snack do you like to share?
  Examples: ice cream, cookies, fruit

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play in the park with her [[slot_toy]].
s2: One sunny day, she flew a bright [[slot_color]] kite.
s3: But then the kite flew up high and disappeared.
s4: [[slot_child]] looked everywhere, but she could not find it.
s5: She asked her friend, [[slot_animal]], for help.
s6: They climbed the tall tree and looked over the grass.
s7: They saw the kite tangled in a pine tree.
s8: Together they gently pulled the kite down.
s9: [[slot_child]] thanked her friend and they shared a [[slot_snack]].
s10: They played happily until the sun set, and the kite flew again.
