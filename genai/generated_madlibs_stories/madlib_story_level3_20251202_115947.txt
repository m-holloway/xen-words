Title: The Lost Toy Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A friendly child and a new animal friend find a lost toy together and share a snack.

Slots:
- slot_child (character_name): A name for the main child character.
  Child prompt: What is your favorite name for a child?
  Examples: Lily, Sam, Mia
- slot_park (park_name): A friendly park name.
  Child prompt: What is the name of a park you like?
  Examples: Sunny Park, Green Meadow
- slot_snack (snack_food): A small snack the child brings.
  Child prompt: What snack would you bring?
  Examples: apple, candy bar, cheese stick
- slot_color (color): A bright color.
  Child prompt: What bright color do you like?
  Examples: red, blue, yellow
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child plays with.
  Child prompt: What toy would you like to play with?
  Examples: ball, doll, kite
- slot_animal (animal_pet): A friendly animal the child meets.
  Child prompt: What friendly animal would you meet?
  Examples: dog, cat, rabbit
- slot_animal_name (animal_name): Name for the animal friend.
  Child prompt: What name would you give the animal?
  Examples: Buddy, Whiskers, Nibbles

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play in [[slot_park]].
s2: One sunny morning, [[slot_child]] packed a [[slot_snack]] for a picnic.
s3: [[slot_child]] brought a bright [[slot_color]] [[slot_toy]].
s4: While running, [[slot_child]] saw a [[slot_animal]] named [[slot_animal_name]].
s5: The [[slot_animal]] wagged its tail and asked for a game.
s6: [[slot_child]] wanted to play hide‑and‑seek with the [[slot_animal]].
s7: [[slot_child]] hid behind a tree, but the [[slot_toy]] slipped from their hands.
s8: The toy rolled into the river and floated away.
s9: [[slot_child]] felt sad but decided to ask the [[slot_animal]] for help.
s10: Together, they followed the toy’s trail of bubbles.
s11: The toy stopped at a shiny rock, and a fish jumped out.
s12: [[slot_child]] and the [[slot_animal]] shared the fish and the [[slot_snack]], and they laughed.
