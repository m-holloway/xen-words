Title: The Missing Puzzle Piece
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child finds a missing puzzle piece and shares it with a classmate.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the main child character
  Child prompt: What is the name of the child in the story?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Noah
- slot_color (color): A bright color
  Child prompt: Name a bright color
  Examples: red, blue, green
- slot_puzzle_piece (object): A puzzle piece
  Child prompt: Name a puzzle piece
  Examples: square, triangle, circle
- slot_object (object): A classroom object
  Child prompt: Name a classroom object
  Examples: desk, chair, board
- slot_classmate (person): A classmate
  Child prompt: Name a classmate
  Examples: Alex, Mia, Jordan
- slot_sound (sound): A soft sound
  Child prompt: Name a soft sound
  Examples: clack, whisper, humming

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to help the class with puzzles.
s2: One rainy day, the class was putting together a big picture.
s3: A small [[slot_color]] piece was missing from the jigsaw.
s4: [[slot_child]] looked for the piece but it was not there.
s5: They noticed a [[slot_puzzle_piece]] under the [[slot_object]].
s6: The piece glowed like a tiny [[slot_color]] star.
s7: [[slot_child]] felt a tug of wanting to keep it.
s8: Instead, [[slot_child]] gave the piece to [[slot_classmate]].
s9: The classmate smiled and placed the piece in the puzzle.
s10: The picture finished and made a soft [[slot_sound]].
s11: Everyone cheered, and [[slot_child]] felt proud.
s12: Now, [[slot_child]] knows sharing makes puzzles complete.
