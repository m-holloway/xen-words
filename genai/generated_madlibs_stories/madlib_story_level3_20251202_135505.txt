Title: The Balloon Dragon Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child and their pet try to bring a floating balloon down, learning kindness and teamwork.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the main character.
  Child prompt: What is your name?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Max
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy or friendly animal the child might like.
  Child prompt: Can you name a friendly animal you like?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child loves to play with.
  Child prompt: What is your favorite toy?
  Examples: ball, doll, blocks
- slot_place (place_name): A place where the child plays.
  Child prompt: Where do you like to play?
  Examples: playground, flower garden, park
- slot_weather (weather): A type of weather the child enjoys.
  Child prompt: What weather do you like?
  Examples: sunny, cloudy, rainy
- slot_color (color): A bright color for the balloon.
  Child prompt: What is a bright color you like?
  Examples: rainbow, purple, orange
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack the child loves.
  Child prompt: What snack do you love?
  Examples: cookies, fruit, granola bar
- slot_activity (activity): A fun activity the child does.
  Child prompt: What fun activity do you do?
  Examples: sing, dance, skip rope

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_pet]] and [[slot_toy]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: One bright [[slot_weather]] day, a shiny [[slot_color]] balloon floated up, like a tiny dragon dancing in the sky.
s3: It twirled and sang, and [[slot_child]] felt a little curious.
s4: The balloon drifted higher, and [[slot_child]] wondered how to reach it.
s5: Almost [[slot_child]] shouted for help, but then remembered the kind words of [[slot_pet]].
s6: Instead, [[slot_child]] whispered, 'Let’s use the [[slot_activity]] to bring it down.'
s7: They tied a bright ribbon to the balloon and let it glide like a friendly kite.
s8: The balloon landed gently on a tree, and [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] shared a tasty [[slot_snack]].
s9: The balloon smiled and floated back, inviting them to play again.
s10: They laughed and danced, and the sky looked brighter.
s11: When the sun set, the balloon floated home, and [[slot_child]] felt proud.
s12: From that day, every [[slot_weather]] day, [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] would look up for new adventures.
