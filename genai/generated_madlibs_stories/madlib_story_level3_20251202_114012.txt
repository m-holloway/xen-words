Title: Playful Picnic in the Park
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child and a friendly animal share a toy, snack, and a fun game in the park.

Slots:
- slot_child (person_name): A name for the main child character.
  Child prompt: Can you give a name for the child?
  Examples: Lily, Max, Sam
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child likes.
  Child prompt: Can you name a toy the child loves?
  Examples: ball, doll, teddy bear
- slot_animal (animal_pet): A friendly animal friend.
  Child prompt: Can you name a friendly animal?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_activity (playground_activity): A playground game to play.
  Child prompt: Can you think of a playground game?
  Examples: tag, hide-and-seek, swing
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack to share.
  Child prompt: Can you name a snack?
  Examples: cookie, sandwich, fruit
- slot_color (color): A color for the blanket.
  Child prompt: Can you name a color?
  Examples: blue, red, green

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the sunny park.
s2: One day, a friendly [[slot_animal]] hopped up to [[slot_child]].
s3: The [[slot_animal]] asked, 'Can we play together?'
s4: [[slot_child]] smiled and said, 'Yes, let’s play a game of [[slot_activity]]!'
s5: They both ran to the big swing set and started to swing high.
s6: After swinging, they sat on the bench and shared a warm [[slot_snack]].
s7: The [[slot_animal]] said, 'That snack tastes great! Thank you, [[slot_child]]!'
s8: [[slot_child]] felt proud to share and to be friends.
s9: When the sun went down, they waved goodbye and promised to meet again.
s10: Back home, [[slot_child]] tucked the toy into a soft [[slot_color]] blanket.
s11: They dreamed of playing [[slot_activity]] again tomorrow.
s12: And they all lived happily, ready for new adventures.
