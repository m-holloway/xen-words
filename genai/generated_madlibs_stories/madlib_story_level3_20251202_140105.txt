Title: The Kind Stick Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A kind child helps a stuck bird in the park using a stick and shares a snack.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): The name of the main child character.
  Child prompt: What is your name?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Max
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child loves to play with.
  Child prompt: What is your favorite toy?
  Examples: teddy bear, rubber duck, play dinosaur
- slot_animal (animal_small): A small animal that appears in the story.
  Child prompt: Name a small animal you like.
  Examples: bird, squirrel, frog
- slot_tool (tool): A tool the child can use.
  Child prompt: What is a long tool you might find?
  Examples: stick, rope, ladder
- slot_color (color): A color used to describe the bird's song.
  Child prompt: Name a color you see often.
  Examples: blue, green, red
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack the child shares.
  Child prompt: What is a snack you like?
  Examples: cookie, apple, sandwich

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the park.
s2: One sunny day, a tiny [[slot_animal]] fluttered down from a tree.
s3: The bird was stuck in a high branch and could not fall.
s4: [[slot_child]] wanted to climb the tree to help.
s5: But the tree was tall, and the climb looked scary.
s6: [[slot_child]] thought of a long [[slot_tool]] that could reach the bird.
s7: He used the stick to gently tap the branch.
s8: The bird sang a bright [[slot_color]] song that sounded like a tiny rainbow.
s9: [[slot_child]] smiled and waved the stick.
s10: The bird hopped down, happy and safe.
s11: [[slot_child]] then shared a [[slot_snack]] with the bird.
s12: The bird thanked [[slot_child]] with a sweet chirp, and they played together for a while.
