Title: The Lost Balloon Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A gentle adventure about finding a missing toy and sharing with a friend.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the main child character
  Child prompt: What is the name of the child?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Alex
- slot_toy (toy_object): A small toy the child likes
  Child prompt: What toy does the child like to play with?
  Examples: balloon, teddy bear, doll
- slot_pet (animal_pet): The child's pet animal
  Child prompt: What pet does the child have?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_color (color): A bright color
  Child prompt: Name a bright color.
  Examples: red, blue, yellow
- slot_friend_name (friend_name): Name of the child's friend
  Child prompt: What is the name of the child's friend?
  Examples: Sam, Mia, Jordan
- slot_activity (playground_activity): An activity at the playground
  Child prompt: What activity do they do at the playground?
  Examples: building a castle, climbing a slide, playing tag
- slot_food (snack_food): A crunchy snack
  Child prompt: What crunchy snack do they eat?
  Examples: chips, crackers, apple slices
- slot_weather (weather): A weather condition
  Child prompt: What weather is it?
  Examples: sunny, cloudy, rainy

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the park.
s2: One sunny day, the [[slot_toy]] was gone!
s3: [[slot_child]] looked under the big [[slot_color]] slide.
s4: Then they saw the [[slot_toy]] floating like a tiny [[slot_color]] sun in the sky.
s5: They reached up, but the toy was too high.
s6: [[slot_child]] almost pushed the toy away, but remembered how [[slot_pet]] liked to share.
s7: So they gently pulled the toy down and gave it to their friend [[slot_friend_name]].
s8: [[slot_friend_name]] giggled and said, 'Thank you! Let's play together.'
s9: They built a castle with blocks and ate a crunchy [[slot_food]] for snack.
s10: The [[slot_weather]] made the day feel like a warm hug.
s11: They laughed and played until the sun set.
