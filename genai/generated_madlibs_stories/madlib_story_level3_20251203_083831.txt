Title: The Blueberry Breakfast Rescue
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child helps fix a milk spill by adding blueberries, turning a kitchen mishap into a tasty new recipe.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the child main character.
  Child prompt: What is a name you like for the kid in this story?
  Examples: Mia, Jamal, Sofia
- slot_adult (family_member): Name of the adult helper.
  Child prompt: What is a name you like for the adult in this story?
  Examples: Mom, Grandpa, Auntie
- slot_dish (food_dish): The specific dish they are making.
  Child prompt: What is a dish you like for them to make?
  Examples: pancakes, pizza, cookies

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] was excited to help [[slot_adult]] make breakfast in the kitchen.
s2: They were making [[slot_dish]] with fluffy batter and a big smile.
s3: Suddenly, the milk spilled all over the counter, and the batter looked sad.
s4: [[slot_child]] thought quickly and said, “Let’s add something sweet!”
s5: The child tossed a handful of blueberries into the batter, and flour puffs into the air like a cloud.
s6: The batter turned a pink, sparkling swirl, and the kitchen smelled like sunshine.
s7: They mixed it all together, and the batter bubbled like a tiny friendly fire.
s8: When they tasted the new [[slot_dish]], they giggled because it was a little strange but yummy.
s9: [[slot_adult]] gave [[slot_child]] a big hug and said, “You made a secret recipe!”
s10: The kitchen felt warm, and they promised to make it again, using more blueberries next time.
