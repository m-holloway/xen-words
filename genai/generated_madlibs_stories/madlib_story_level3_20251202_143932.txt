Title: Lily and the Kite in the Tree
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A gentle story about Lily, a kite stuck in a tree, and how a kind squirrel helps her, showing friendship and teamwork.

Slots:
- slot_child (person_name): The name of the main child character.
  Child prompt: Can you name a child you would like to be friends with?
  Examples: Lily, Tommy, Emma
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child likes to play with.
  Child prompt: Can you name a toy you like to play with?
  Examples: kite, ball, puzzle
- slot_color (color): A bright color.
  Child prompt: Can you name a bright color?
  Examples: purple, red, green
- slot_squirrel (animal_pet): A friendly animal you might see in the park.
  Child prompt: Can you name a friendly animal you might see in the park?
  Examples: squirrel, bird, rabbit
- slot_rope (tool_object): A small rope or string.
  Child prompt: Can you name a small rope or string you might use?
  Examples: rope, string, twine
- slot_snack (snack_food): A tasty snack.
  Child prompt: Can you name a tasty snack you like?
  Examples: candy apples, fruit, cookies

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the park.
s2: One sunny day, the kite was bright [[slot_color]] and danced in the blue sky.
s3: When a gentle wind blew, the kite slipped from [[slot_child]]'s hands and flew up into a tall oak tree.
s4: She looked down and saw the kite stuck among the branches.
s5: She almost climbed the tree to get it, but then she thought about being safe.
s6: She ran over to the friendly squirrel [[slot_squirrel]] who lived near the tree.
s7: The squirrel said, 'I can help! I have a little rope [[slot_rope]] that can pull the kite down.'
s8: Together they tied the rope to the kite and pulled gently.
s9: The kite fluttered like a rainbow butterfly, bright and beautiful.
s10: When it landed, [[slot_child]] and the squirrel shared a snack of [[slot_snack]] and laughed.
s11: The kite flew again, and [[slot_child]] waved goodbye to the squirrel.
s12: She felt happy that she had helped the kite and made a new friend.
s13: Every time she visited the park, she would bring her kite and share it with the squirrel.
s14: The park became a place of friendship and bright colors.
s15: And the kite always remembered how a kind hand helped it soar.
