Title: Mia and the Little Bird
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: Mia sees a tiny bird with a broken wing, thinks about how to help, and learns kindness through gentle action.

Slots:
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy you like to play with
  Child prompt: Can you name a toy you like?
  Examples: ball, doll, block
- slot_place (place_name): A place you go to play
  Child prompt: Can you name a place you like to play at?
  Examples: park, garden, beach
- slot_color (color): A bright color
  Child prompt: Can you name a bright color?
  Examples: pink, blue, yellow
- slot_friend (animal_pet): A friendly animal friend
  Child prompt: Can you name a friendly animal you like?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_snack (snack_food): A favorite snack
  Child prompt: Can you name a snack you like?
  Examples: apple, granola bar, pretzel
- slot_sound (sound): A pleasant sound
  Child prompt: Can you name a sound you like?
  Examples: chirp, rumble, whistle

s1: Mia loved to play with [[slot_toy]] on [[slot_place]].
s2: One day, she saw a tiny bird drooping its wing.
s3: The bird's wing was as soft as a [[slot_color]] feather.
s4: Mia felt sad and wanted to help.
s5: She thought about [[slot_friend]] might know what to do.
s6: She almost tossed the bird onto the ground, but then remembered the story about kindness.
s7: Instead, she tucked the bird into a small blanket and whispered, 'Take care.'
s8: The next day, Mia brought [[slot_snack]] and shared it with the bird.
s9: The bird sang a bright [[slot_sound]] that made Mia smile.
