Title: The Lost Toy Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A gentle adventure where a child learns to ask for help and finds a lost toy with a friendly pet.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the child character
  Child prompt: Can you give a name for the child?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Max
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child likes
  Child prompt: What is a toy you love to play with?
  Examples: balloon, kite, toy car
- slot_place (place_name): A place where the child plays
  Child prompt: Where does the child play?
  Examples: park, garden, beach
- slot_color (color): A bright color
  Child prompt: What is a bright color you like?
  Examples: yellow, blue, purple
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A friendly animal friend
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: bunny, puppy, kitten
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack the child enjoys
  Child prompt: What is a snack you like to eat?
  Examples: cookies, fruit, pretzels

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: One sunny day, the [[slot_toy]] floated up into the sky like a tiny bright cloud.
s3: It drifted over the [[slot_color]] trees and disappeared behind a cloud.
s4: [[slot_child]] felt sad and wanted to run home.
s5: But then [[slot_child]] thought, "I can ask my [[slot_pet]] for help."
s6: [[slot_pet]] sniffed the air and followed a rainbow trail.
s7: The trail led to a hidden pond where the [[slot_toy]] bobbed in the water.
s8: [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] lifted the toy out together.
s9: They sat on a blanket and ate [[slot_snack]] while watching the sun set.
s10: From then on, [[slot_child]] always asked for help when something was lost.
