Title:  The Little Lost Treasure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child finds a shiny object outside and has to decide whether to keep it or return it to a worried animal friend.

Slots:
- slot_name (person_name): The main character's name.
  Child prompt: What is the name of a brave boy or girl?
  Examples: Leo, Maya, Sam
- slot_place (place_outdoor): A specific outdoor location.
  Child prompt: Name a place outside where you like to play.
  Examples: playground, forest, garden
- slot_color (color): A color describing the object.
  Child prompt: What is your favorite bright color?
  Examples: silver, purple, bright blue
- slot_object (small_object): A small item a child might find.
  Child prompt: Name something small you might keep in your pocket.
  Examples: marble, button, key
- slot_animal (animal_forest): A small animal.
  Child prompt: Name a small, fuzzy animal.
  Examples: squirrel, mouse, bunny
- slot_feeling (adjective_emotion): An emotion (negative/concerned).
  Child prompt: How do you look when you lose your favorite toy?
  Examples: worried, sad, scared
- slot_movement (movement_noun): A movement word used as a noun (e.g., a jump).
  Child prompt: Name a funny move, like a 'wiggle' or a 'hop'.
  Examples: flip, dance, shimmy
- slot_snack (food_snack): A treat.
  Child prompt: What is a yummy snack?
  Examples: cookie, strawberry, cracker

Story template:
s1: One sunny morning, [[slot_name]] went for a walk in the [[slot_place]].
s2: Something [[slot_color]] sparkled near a big rock.
s3: It was a tiny [[slot_object]], smooth and shiny.
s4: [[slot_name]] picked it up and squeezed it tight.
s5: "I will keep this treasure forever," [[slot_name]] whispered.
s6: Suddenly, a soft noise came from the bushes.
s7: A little [[slot_animal]] peeked out from behind a fern.
s8: The creature looked very [[slot_feeling]].
s9: It was sniffing the ground, looking for something important.
s10: [[slot_name]] looked at the shiny treasure, then back at the sad animal.
s11: It was hard to let go, but [[slot_name]] gently opened their hand.
s12: The animal’s eyes went wide with surprise.
s13: It grabbed the treasure and did a happy [[slot_movement]].
s14: To say thank you, the creature pushed a [[slot_snack]] toward [[slot_name]].
s15: [[slot_name]] smiled, because sharing felt even better than finding treasure.
