Title: The Marble and the Moonlit Snail
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A kind child helps a snail and finds a glowing pebble.

Slots:
- slot_name (child_name): Name of the main character
  Child prompt: Can you think of a name for the main character?
  Examples: Lily, Max, Sam
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy stuffed animal the child might like
  Child prompt: Can you name a stuffed animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: Bunny, Puppy, Kitten
- slot_toy (toy_object): A favorite toy the child loves
  Child prompt: Can you name a toy you like to play with?
  Examples: Marble, Doll, Puzzle
- slot_sandbox (location): Name of the sandbox at the park
  Child prompt: Can you think of a name for a sandbox?
  Examples: Sunny Sandbox, Blue Bin, Green Box
- slot_pebble (object): A small glowing pebble
  Child prompt: Can you think of a small glowing pebble?
  Examples: glowing pebble, sparkling stone, tiny star
- slot_friend (child_name): Name of a friend the child meets
  Child prompt: Can you think of a name for a friend?
  Examples: Emma, Noah, Olivia

Story template:
s1: [[slot_name]] loved to play with [[slot_pet]] and [[slot_toy]] on sunny days.
s2: One morning, they went to the park and saw a big sandbox called [[slot_sandbox]].
s3: When [[slot_name]] rolled [[slot_toy]] into the sandbox, it disappeared under the sand.
s4: [[slot_name]] felt sad and searched for the marble.
s5: Suddenly, a tiny snail with a shell that looked like a [[slot_pebble]] slid by.
s6: The snail was slow, and [[slot_name]] thought about pushing it away.
s7: But [[slot_name]] remembered how [[slot_pet]] liked to help others, so they gently lifted the snail.
s8: They followed the snail to a bright flower pot where the snail's home was.
s9: The snail thanked [[slot_name]] by giving a glowing [[slot_pebble]] that sparkled like a tiny star.
s10: [[slot_name]] shared the glowing pebble with [[slot_friend]] and they both giggled.
s11: Later, [[slot_name]] and [[slot_pet]] played with the marble again, and the pebble shone beside them.
s12: They learned that caring for a small friend can bring bright surprises.
