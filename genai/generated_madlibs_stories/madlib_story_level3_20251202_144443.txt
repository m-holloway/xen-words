Title: Lily and the Hummingbird in the Garden
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: Lily helps a shy hummingbird find its way, learning kindness and patience.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the child character
  Child prompt: Can you give a name for the child?
  Examples: Lily, Sam, Mia
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy or friendly animal the child might like
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_flower (flower): A type of flower in the garden
  Child prompt: Can you name a flower that grows in gardens?
  Examples: rose, tulip, daisy
- slot_nest_material (material): Something the child can use to make a nest
  Child prompt: Can you think of something soft that could be used as a nest?
  Examples: cotton, leaf, grass
- slot_feather_color (color): Color of a feather
  Child prompt: Can you name a bright color?
  Examples: red, blue, yellow
- slot_garden_item (garden_item): An item in the garden
  Child prompt: Can you name something you might see in a garden?
  Examples: watering can, shovel, bench

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play in the backyard garden.
s2: One sunny morning, [[slot_child]] saw a [[slot_pet]] hopping near a [[slot_flower]].
s3: The [[slot_pet]] was shy, and a tiny hummingbird was stuck in the petals.
s4: The hummingbird tried to fly, but it couldn't get out.
s5: [[slot_child]] decided to help the hummingbird.
s6: She gathered a soft [[slot_nest_material]] and made a little nest.
s7: The hummingbird rested, and its feathers shone like a [[slot_feather_color]] star.
s8: Soon, the hummingbird was ready to fly again.
s9: It fluttered out of the flower, thanked [[slot_child]] with a bright chirp, and disappeared.
s10: Later, a single [[slot_feather_color]] feather fell to the ground, and [[slot_child]] placed it on the garden bench.
