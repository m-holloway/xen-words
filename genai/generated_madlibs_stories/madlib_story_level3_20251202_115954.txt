Title: The Missing Snack Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child forgets a snack and finds it with a friendly animal, sharing it with a friend.

Slots:
- slot_child_name (child_name): Name of the child character
  Child prompt: What is your favorite name for a child?
  Examples: Lily, Tommy, Mia
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child likes
  Child prompt: What toy do you like to play with?
  Examples: ball, teddy bear, toy car
- slot_color (color): A bright color
  Child prompt: What bright color do you like?
  Examples: red, blue, yellow
- slot_friend (friend_name): Name of a friend
  Child prompt: What is the name of a friend you like?
  Examples: Sam, Zoe, Ben
- slot_animal (animal_pet): A friendly animal
  Child prompt: Name a friendly animal you would like to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack the child can eat
  Child prompt: What snack do you like?
  Examples: apple slices, pretzel sticks, candy
- slot_activity (playground_activity): An activity at the playground
  Child prompt: What playground activity do you enjoy?
  Examples: swing, slide, climbing frame

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child_name]] loved to play in the park.
s2: One sunny day, [[slot_child_name]] brought a [[slot_toy]] and a bright [[slot_color]] backpack.
s3: [[slot_child_name]] wanted to share a snack with [[slot_friend]].
s4: But [[slot_child_name]] forgot to put the snack inside the backpack.
s5: [[slot_child_name]] looked everywhere, but the snack was missing.
s6: [[slot_child_name]] asked [[slot_animal]] for help.
s7: [[slot_animal]] nudged the backpack and found a small [[slot_snack]] hidden under a leaf.
s8: [[slot_child_name]] and [[slot_friend]] ate the snack together.
s9: They laughed and played on the [[slot_activity]].
s10: At the end of the day, [[slot_child_name]] felt happy and tired.
