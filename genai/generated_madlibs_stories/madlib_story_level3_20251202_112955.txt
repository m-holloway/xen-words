Title: The Lost Toy Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A warm story about a child, a pet, and a small adventure that ends happily.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): The name of the main character.
  Child prompt: What is your name?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Noah
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A friendly animal companion.
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_toy (toy): A favorite toy the child plays with.
  Child prompt: What is your favorite toy?
  Examples: ball, teddy bear, blocks
- slot_color (color): A bright color.
  Child prompt: What is a bright color you like?
  Examples: yellow, blue, pink
- slot_mood (emotion): How the child feels at the start.
  Child prompt: How are you feeling today?
  Examples: happy, curious, excited
- slot_place (location): A place the child visits.
  Child prompt: Where do you like to go?
  Examples: park, beach, garden
- slot_action (verb): An action the child does.
  Child prompt: What do you like to do?
  Examples: run, jump, play
- slot_problem (noun): A small, gentle problem.
  Child prompt: What is a small problem you might have?
  Examples: lost ball, missing shoe, paper cut

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] felt [[slot_mood]] this morning.
s2: He/she had a [[slot_pet]] and a [[slot_toy]] that loved to play with.
s3: In the [[slot_place]], they [[slot_action]] and laughed.
s4: Suddenly, the [[slot_toy]] was gone!
s5: There was a small problem: the [[slot_problem]].
s6: But [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] decided to look for it.
s7: They searched under the [[slot_color]] tree and behind the [[slot_color]] bench.
s8: Finally, they found the [[slot_toy]] tucked inside a [[slot_color]] box.
s9: They were happy and gave each other a hug.
s10: The day ended with a bright sunset over the [[slot_place]].
s11: They learned that sharing and looking together solves problems.
s12: And they promised to play again tomorrow.
