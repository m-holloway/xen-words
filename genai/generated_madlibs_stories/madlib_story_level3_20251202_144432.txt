Title: The Lost Stuffed Animal
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child finds a lost stuffed animal on the bus and helps its owner get it back.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the main child character
  Child prompt: What is the name of the child in the story?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Noah
- slot_color (color): A bright color
  Child prompt: Name a bright color
  Examples: red, blue, green
- slot_stuffed_animal (toy_animal): A stuffed animal
  Child prompt: Name a stuffed animal
  Examples: teddy bear, bunny, dragon
- slot_bus_seat (object): A bus seat
  Child prompt: Name a bus seat
  Examples: front seat, back seat, window seat
- slot_sound (sound): A soft sound
  Child prompt: Name a soft sound
  Examples: ding, whisper, humming

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved riding the bus to school.
s2: On the bus, a [[slot_color]] stuffed animal fell into a seat cushion.
s3: The animal looked sad because it could not find its owner.
s4: [[slot_child]] wanted to keep it, but the animal was lonely.
s5: First, [[slot_child]] looked around the bus but no one was near the cushion.
s6: Then, [[slot_child]] remembered the back seat cushion where the animal was.
s7: They lifted the cushion and the animal's eyes sparkled like tiny [[slot_color]] fireflies.
s8: [[slot_child]] felt a tug of wanting to keep it.
s9: Instead, [[slot_child]] asked the teacher, 'Who owns this animal?'
s10: The teacher smiled and said the animal belonged to a classmate.
s11: The classmate thanked [[slot_child]] and gave the animal a big hug.
s12: The bus ride ended with a soft [[slot_sound]] of the bell.
s13: Now, [[slot_child]] knows sharing makes friends happy.
