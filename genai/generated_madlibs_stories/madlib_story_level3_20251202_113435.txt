Title: The Lost Ball Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A friendly child and their pet find a lost ball with a gentle problem helped by a helpful friend.

Slots:
- slot_name (child_name): The main character's name
  Child prompt: What is your name?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Mia
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy or friendly animal the child might like
  Child prompt: Can you name a pet you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_toy (toy): A favorite toy the child plays with
  Child prompt: What is your favorite toy?
  Examples: ball, teddy bear, doll
- slot_item (object): An item that gets lost
  Child prompt: What is something you might lose?
  Examples: cup, shoe, hat
- slot_friend (friend): A friendly companion
  Child prompt: What is the name of a friend you like to play with?
  Examples: Tommy, Anna, Josh
- slot_place (place): A place where the adventure happens
  Child prompt: Where do you like to play?
  Examples: park, garden, beach

Story template:
s1: [[slot_name]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: One sunny day, [[slot_pet]] barked happily as they chased the [[slot_toy]].
s3: Suddenly, the [[slot_toy]] slipped from [[slot_name]]'s hands and rolled away.
s4: [[slot_name]] looked around and saw the [[slot_toy]] missing.
s5: They asked [[slot_friend]] if they saw it.
s6: [[slot_friend]] said, "I saw it under the big tree!"
s7: Together they ran to the tree and found the [[slot_toy]] resting on a leaf.
s8: [[slot_name]] was happy and thanked [[slot_friend]] and [[slot_pet]] for helping.
s9: They all played again, this time keeping the [[slot_toy]] close.
s10: At the end of the day, they curled up on the grass and dreamed of new adventures.
