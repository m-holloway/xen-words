Title: The Humming Kite Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A kind child helps a humming kite get back to its owner, sharing a snack and making a new friend.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): The name of the main character.
  Child prompt: Can you give a name for the child who loves to play?
  Examples: Lily, Sam, Mia
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child enjoys playing with.
  Child prompt: What toy does the child like to play with?
  Examples: paper kite, soft doll, toy car
- slot_kite_color (color): The color of the kite.
  Child prompt: What color is the kite?
  Examples: red, blue, green
- slot_musical_sound (sound): A musical sound the kite makes.
  Child prompt: What sound does the kite make?
  Examples: twinkle, hum, chirp
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack the child shares.
  Child prompt: What snack does the child share?
  Examples: banana, cookie, candy
- slot_activity (playground_activity): An activity the child does in the playground.
  Child prompt: What playground activity does the child do?
  Examples: slide, swing, climb
- slot_owner_name (person_name): The name of the kite's owner.
  Child prompt: What is the kite's owner's name?
  Examples: Emma, Noah, Olivia
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy or friendly animal the child might like.
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the park.
s2: One sunny day, [[slot_child]] saw a shiny [[slot_kite_color]] kite humming in the sky.
s3: The kite was stuck high in a tall tree, and it made a gentle [[slot_musical_sound]].
s4: [[slot_child]] wondered who the kite belonged to.
s5: The [[slot_pet]] hopped beside [[slot_child]] and gave a friendly bark.
s6: At first, [[slot_child]] thought maybe the kite would just fall down soon.
s7: But then [[slot_child]] remembered the kind voice of the kite, and decided to help.
s8: [[slot_child]] climbed the [[slot_activity]] slowly, holding onto the branches.
s9: When [[slot_child]] reached the kite, it was warm and friendly.
s10: With a gentle pull, [[slot_child]] brought the kite down to the ground.
s11: The kite's owner, [[slot_owner_name]], ran over and thanked [[slot_child]] with a big smile.
s12: To celebrate, the kite sang a bright [[slot_musical_sound]] that sounded like a xylophone.
s13: [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] shared a [[slot_snack]] under the tree.
s14: They watched the kite flutter like a rainbow feather in the wind.
s15: The day ended with laughter and a new friendship.
s16: And [[slot_child]] promised to visit the park again soon.
