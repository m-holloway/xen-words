Title: The Happy Toy and the Kind Friend
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child finds a sad toy, decides to help it, and together they bring joy to the playground.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): The name of the main child character.
  Child prompt: Can you give a name for the child?
  Examples: Lily, Tom, Mia
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy or friendly animal the child might like.
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_toy (toy_object): A small toy that the child can play with.
  Child prompt: Can you name a toy that you like?
  Examples: doll, car, ball
- slot_color (color): A bright color to describe the toy.
  Child prompt: What is a bright color you like?
  Examples: pink, orange, green
- slot_snack (snack_food): A tasty snack the child can share.
  Child prompt: Can you name a snack you like?
  Examples: cookies, fruit, popcorn
- slot_place (place_name): A place where the playground is located.
  Child prompt: Where is the playground?
  Examples: city park, suburban park, schoolyard
- slot_activity (playground_activity): An activity the child can do at the playground.
  Child prompt: What is a playground activity you like?
  Examples: tag, slide, climb

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_pet]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: One sunny day, they saw a [[slot_color]] [[slot_toy]] that looked sad.
s3: The toy was lonely because it had no friends to play with.
s4: [[slot_child]] thought, 'Maybe I can make it happy.'
s5: [[slot_child]] wondered if they should just leave the toy alone, but they chose to help.
s6: First, [[slot_child]] tried to sing a song, but the toy stayed quiet.
s7: Then [[slot_child]] brought a [[slot_snack]] to share, hoping the toy would smile.
s8: The toy's eyes twinkled, and it started to hum like a tiny drum.
s9: That humming made the whole playground light up with a soft glow.
s10: [[slot_child]] decided to invite the [[slot_pet]] to play with the toy.
s11: Together, they played [[slot_activity]] and the toy laughed, humming louder.
s12: The [[slot_color]] toy glowed brighter, like a sunrise on a cloud.
s13: When the sun set, the toy said, 'Thank you, friend,' and they all went home happy.
