Title: The Missing Star Puzzle
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A gentle story about a child finding a missing puzzle piece with the help of a friendly animal.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the main child character.
  Child prompt: What is your name?
  Examples: Lily, Max, Sam
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy that the child plays with.
  Child prompt: What is your favorite toy?
  Examples: ball, doll, puzzle
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A friendly animal friend.
  Child prompt: What is the name of a friendly animal you like?
  Examples: Bobby the dog, Milo the rabbit, Kitty the kitten
- slot_snack (snack_food): A crunchy snack they share.
  Child prompt: What is a crunchy snack you enjoy?
  Examples: chips, crackers, apple slices

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the park.
s2: One day, the toy was a picture of the sun, but one bright yellow star piece was missing.
s3: The child looked around and wondered where the piece had gone.
s4: She almost decided to throw the puzzle away, but then thought of her friend [[slot_pet]].
s5: She called out, "Hello [[slot_pet]], can you see the missing star?"
s6: The [[slot_pet]] sniffed the air and trotted to the big oak tree.
s7: Under the tree, the star piece glowed like a tiny sunrise.
s8: The child reached out and placed the piece into the picture.
s9: The sun picture shone brighter, and the park felt warmer.
s10: They celebrated with a crunchy [[slot_snack]] and laughed together.
