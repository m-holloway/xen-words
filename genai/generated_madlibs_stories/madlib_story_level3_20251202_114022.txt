Title: The Lost Toy Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child and a friendly pet find a lost toy in the park, learning teamwork and sharing.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): The name of the main child character.
  Child prompt: What is your name?
  Examples: Lily, Max, Sam
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child likes to play with.
  Child prompt: What toy do you love to play with?
  Examples: ball, doll, teddy bear
- slot_park (park_name): The name of the park or playground.
  Child prompt: What is the name of the park you like?
  Examples: Sunny Park, Greenwood Playground, Riverbend
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy or friendly animal the child might like.
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_color (color): A bright color.
  Child prompt: What is a bright color you like?
  Examples: red, blue, yellow
- slot_snack (snack_food): A small snack the child enjoys.
  Child prompt: What snack do you like?
  Examples: apple, cookie, granola bar
- slot_friend (friend_name): The name of a friend the child meets.
  Child prompt: What is your friend's name?
  Examples: Sam, Jenna, Leo
- slot_activity (playground_activity): An activity played at the playground.
  Child prompt: What playground activity do you like?
  Examples: swing, slide, slide

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in [[slot_park]].
s2: One sunny day, [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] went to [[slot_park]].
s3: [[slot_child]] rolled the [[slot_toy]] high and low.
s4: Suddenly, the [[slot_toy]] slipped out of [[slot_child]]'s hands.
s5: It rolled into the big [[slot_color]] tunnel.
s6: [[slot_child]] felt a little sad.
s7: [[slot_pet]] sniffed around and found a small [[slot_snack]].
s8: [[slot_pet]] said, "I can help you find it!"
s9: Together, they followed the tunnel.
s10: They met [[slot_friend]] who was playing on the [[slot_activity]].
s11: [[slot_friend]] said, "I saw the [[slot_toy]] near the swing."
s12: [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] ran to the swing.
s13: The [[slot_toy]] was stuck in a bush.
s14: [[slot_child]] gently pulled it out.
s15: They all cheered and shared the [[slot_snack]].
s16: From then on, they played happily together.
