Title: Lila's Little Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A kind child loses a toy and finds it with the help of a pet, learning to be careful and grateful.

Slots:
- slot_child (character_name): Name of the child character.
  Child prompt: What is your name?
  Examples: Lila, Sam, Mia
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy or friendly animal the child might like.
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_toy (toy): A small toy the child plays with.
  Child prompt: What is your favorite toy?
  Examples: rabbit, doll, teddy bear
- slot_place (place): A location in the park where the adventure happens.
  Child prompt: Where do you like to go in the park?
  Examples: garden, treehouse, pond
- slot_sound (sound): A gentle sound that guides the child.
  Child prompt: What is a soft sound you hear in nature?
  Examples: whisper, chirp, rumble
- slot_color (color): A bright color that describes the setting.
  Child prompt: What is a bright color you like?
  Examples: red, blue, yellow
- slot_noun (noun): A noun that describes something in the park.
  Child prompt: What is a plant or tree you see?
  Examples: flower, bush, tree
- slot_action (verb): An action the child does.
  Child prompt: What do you do when you find something?
  Examples: search, look, pick

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] at the park.
s2: One sunny day, [[slot_child]] went to the park with [[slot_pet]].
s3: [[slot_child]] laughed and chased [[slot_toy]] around the trees.
s4: Suddenly, [[slot_toy]] disappeared! [[slot_child]] looked everywhere.
s5: [[slot_child]] asked [[slot_pet]] for help.
s6: [[slot_pet]] sniffed the air and said, "I hear a [[slot_sound]] coming from the [[slot_place]]!"
s7: [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] followed the sound.
s8: They reached a [[slot_color]] [[slot_noun]] in the [[slot_place]].
s9: [[slot_child]] found [[slot_toy]] tucked under the leaf.
s10: [[slot_child]] hugged [[slot_toy]] and thanked [[slot_pet]].
s11: They both laughed and promised to always keep [[slot_toy]] safe.
s12: From that day, [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] had many more adventures together.
