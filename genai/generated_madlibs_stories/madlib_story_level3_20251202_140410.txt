Title: The Missing Toy Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A friendly child searches for a missing toy and shares it with a new friend, learning kindness through play.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the main child character
  Child prompt: Can you give a name for the main character?
  Examples: Lily, Sam, Mia
- slot_toy (toy_object): Favorite toy the child plays with
  Child prompt: Name a toy you like to play with.
  Examples: rubber duck, teddy bear, ball
- slot_place (place_name): Where the story happens
  Child prompt: Name a place you love to visit.
  Examples: park, yard, garden
- slot_playground (playground_activity): Activity at the playground
  Child prompt: Name a playground activity.
  Examples: slide, see-saw, climbing frame
- slot_color (color): A bright color
  Child prompt: Name a bright color.
  Examples: green, red, blue
- slot_neighbor (neighbor_name): Name of a friendly neighbor
  Child prompt: Name a friendly neighbor.
  Examples: Mr. Jones, Mrs. Lee, Sammy
- slot_animal (animal_pet): A small animal friend
  Child prompt: Name a small animal you like.
  Examples: bunny, kitten, puppy

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: One sunny morning, the [[slot_toy]] was gone.
s3: [[slot_child]] felt a little sad, but decided to look for it.
s4: First, [[slot_child]] checked the [[slot_playground]].
s5: The [[slot_playground]] was busy, and the [[slot_toy]] was not there.
s6: Next, [[slot_child]] asked [[slot_neighbor]] if they had seen the toy.
s7: [[slot_neighbor]] smiled and said, "I saw a bright [[slot_color]] backpack on the bench."
s8: [[slot_child]] ran to the bench and opened the backpack.
s9: Inside, the [[slot_toy]] was tucked inside a soft [[slot_animal]] plush.
s10: [[slot_child]] felt the toy hug and thanked the backpack.
s11: Then, [[slot_child]] shared the [[slot_toy]] with [[slot_animal]]'s friend, a small [[slot_animal]].
s12: They all played together, and the [[slot_place]] rang with laughter.
