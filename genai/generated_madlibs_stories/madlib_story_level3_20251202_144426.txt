Title: The Kind Butterfly in the Garden
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child helps a shy butterfly find its way home in the garden.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the main child character
  Child prompt: What is the name of the child in the story?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Noah
- slot_flower (plant_flower): Name of a flower
  Child prompt: Name a flower you see in the garden
  Examples: Rose, Sunflower, Daisy
- slot_color (color): A bright color
  Child prompt: Name a bright color
  Examples: red, blue, green
- slot_tool (object): A small tool the child might have
  Child prompt: Name a small tool you might carry
  Examples: net, stick, bucket
- slot_sound (sound): A soft sound
  Child prompt: Name a soft sound
  Examples: buzz, whisper, humming

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play in the backyard garden.
s2: One sunny morning, a [[slot_color]] butterfly fluttered near the [[slot_flower]].
s3: The butterfly looked sad because it could not find its home.
s4: [[slot_child]] wanted to help, but the butterfly was shy.
s5: First, [[slot_child]] tried to touch the butterfly, but it fluttered away.
s6: Then, [[slot_child]] remembered a small [[slot_tool]] in the pocket.
s7: Using the net, [[slot_child]] gently guided the butterfly to a bright [[slot_flower]].
s8: The butterfly landed and its wings shone like a [[slot_color]] rainbow.
s9: It sang a soft [[slot_sound]] that made the garden feel calm.
s10: [[slot_child]] smiled and felt warm inside.
s11: The butterfly thanked [[slot_child]] with a flutter and flew home.
s12: Now, whenever [[slot_child]] sees a butterfly, they remember to be kind.
