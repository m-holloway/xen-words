Title: The Little Bell‑Bird and the Kind Visitor
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child helps a tiny, singing bird and shares a snack, learning a gentle lesson about kindness.

Slots:
- slot_child (animal_name): The name of the main child character.
  Child prompt: Can you give a name for the child in the story?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Noah
- slot_toy (toy_object): A favorite toy that the child plays with.
  Child prompt: What is a toy the child loves?
  Examples: plush dinosaur, soft rabbit, toy car
- slot_color (color): The color of the bird.
  Child prompt: What color is the bird?
  Examples: blue, green, yellow
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack the child shares with the bird.
  Child prompt: What snack does the child give?
  Examples: apple slice, candy bar, crackers
- slot_friend (person_name): A friend who also enjoys the bird's song.
  Child prompt: Name a friend who likes the bird too.
  Examples: Mia, Ethan, Olivia
- slot_place (place_name): A place where the bird visits often.
  Child prompt: Where does the bird visit?
  Examples: garden, backyard, playground

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the park.
s2: One sunny day, they saw a tiny [[slot_color]] bird perched on a high branch.
s3: The bird sang a song that sounded like a little bell.
s4: [[slot_child]] wondered if the bird could get down.
s5: They tried to shout, but the bird stayed put.
s6: [[slot_child]] almost walked away, thinking the bird would be fine.
s7: Then they remembered the kind rule they learned at school: help a friend.
s8: [[slot_child]] carefully climbed the tree and reached the bird.
s9: The bird hopped onto [[slot_toy]]'s back and flew down.
s10: [[slot_child]] shared a bite of [[slot_snack]] with the bird.
s11: The bird chirped happily and thanked [[slot_child]] with a bright smile.
s12: From then on, the bird visited every [[slot_place]] and sang for [[slot_child]] and [[slot_friend]].
