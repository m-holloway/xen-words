Title: The Balloon Pet Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A kind child helps a lost balloon‑tied pet find its home in the park.

Slots:
- slot_child (person_name): The name of the main child character.
  Child prompt: Can you tell me the name of the child in the story?
  Examples: Mia, Liam, Sofia
- slot_place (place_name): The name of the park where the story happens.
  Child prompt: What is the name of the park?
  Examples: Sunny Park, Riverbend Park, Maple Grove
- slot_color (color): The color of the balloon.
  Child prompt: What color is the balloon?
  Examples: blue, orange, purple
- slot_pet (animal_pet): The type of animal tied to the balloon.
  Child prompt: What animal is tied to the balloon?
  Examples: rabbit, hamster, duck
- slot_snack (snack_food): The snack the child brings.
  Child prompt: What snack does the child bring?
  Examples: apple slice, candy cane, pretzel
- slot_toy (toy_object): The toy the child almost takes home.
  Child prompt: What toy does the child have?
  Examples: ball, doll, blocks
- slot_neighbor (person_name): The name of the friendly neighbor.
  Child prompt: What is the neighbor's name?
  Examples: Mrs. Patel, Mr. Gomez, Nina

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play in [[slot_place]] on sunny days.
s2: One morning, [[slot_child]] saw a tiny [[slot_color]] balloon bobbing in the grass.
s3: The balloon was tied to a small [[slot_pet]] that looked lost and shy.
s4: [[slot_child]] felt a tug in the heart and wondered where the [[slot_pet]] came from.
s5: The child almost ran home to get [[slot_toy]], but decided to stay and help.
s6: [[slot_child]] whispered, “I will find you home,” and put a soft [[slot_snack]] on the ground.
s7: The [[slot_pet]] sniffed the snack and followed the scent.
s8: Together they walked past the fountain and the big oak tree.
s9: A friendly [[slot_neighbor]] noticed the balloon and asked, “Are you looking for someone?”
s10: [[slot_child]] explained the situation, and [[slot_neighbor]] remembered seeing a [[slot_pet]] with a blue collar near the playground.
s11: They all followed the trail of the blue collar.
s12: At the playground, a little boy waved and said, “That’s my [[slot_pet]]!” He laughed and thanked [[slot_child]].
s13: [[slot_child]] felt warm inside, like a sunrise.
s14: The balloon floated up and disappeared into the sky, leaving a bright memory.
s15: From then on, [[slot_child]] always kept a snack for friends in the park.
s16: And every day, the park smelled of adventure and kindness.
