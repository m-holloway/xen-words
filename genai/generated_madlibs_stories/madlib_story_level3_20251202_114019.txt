Title: Milo's Missing Balloon
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A gentle story about Milo, a missing balloon, and friends helping to find it.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): A name for the main child character.
  Child prompt: Can you name a child who might be in the story?
  Examples: Milo, Emma, Tommy
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy that the child loves.
  Child prompt: Can you name a toy you like to play with?
  Examples: balloon, doll, teddy bear
- slot_color (color): Color of the balloon.
  Child prompt: What is a bright color?
  Examples: red, blue, yellow
- slot_place (place_name): A place where the play happens.
  Child prompt: Can you name a place where kids play?
  Examples: park, garden, playground
- slot_friend (friend_name): A friend who helps.
  Child prompt: Can you name a friend?
  Examples: Lily, Sam, Olivia
- slot_activity (playground_activity): An activity they do to find the balloon.
  Child prompt: Can you name a game kids play?
  Examples: hide-and-seek, tag, ring toss
- slot_snack (snack_food): A tasty food they share.
  Child prompt: What is a snack you like?
  Examples: cookies, fruit, granola bar
- slot_song (song_name): A song they sing together.
  Child prompt: Can you name a kids' song?
  Examples: Twinkle Twinkle, Old McDonald, The Wheels on the Bus

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: One day, the [[slot_toy]] floated away.
s3: [[slot_child]] felt sad and looked around.
s4: He called his friend [[slot_friend]] for help.
s5: They decided to play [[slot_activity]] to find the balloon.
s6: While searching, they found a shiny [[slot_color]] [[slot_toy]] under a tree.
s7: [[slot_child]] was happy and thanked [[slot_friend]].
s8: They shared a tasty [[slot_snack]] and sang a song.
s9: They sang the [[slot_song]] together.
s10: [[slot_child]] learned that friends help find lost toys.
