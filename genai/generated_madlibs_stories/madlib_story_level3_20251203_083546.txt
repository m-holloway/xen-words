Title: The Plant Doctor's Song
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A kind child helps a sad plant feel better with fun experiments, water, light, and a special song.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Name of the child main character.
  Child prompt: What is a name you like for the kid in this story?
  Examples: Mia, Jamal, Sofia
- slot_plant (plant_type): Type of plant that needs help.
  Child prompt: What kind of plant would you like to help?
  Examples: cactus, fern, sunflower
- slot_cure_action (verb_activity): A silly activity the child tries to cheer the plant.
  Child prompt: What fun thing could you do to make a plant smile?
  Examples: singing, dancing, telling jokes
- slot_music_type (music_genre): Music style the plant listens to.
  Child prompt: What kind of music would you play for the plant?
  Examples: rock and roll, opera, lullabies
- slot_change (visual_change): A sign that the plant is getting better.
  Child prompt: What small change would show the plant is happy again?
  Examples: green leaf, tiny flower, straight stem

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] looked at the plant on the windowsill and felt a little sad.
s2: The [[slot_plant]] had drooping leaves and seemed thirsty.
s3: [[slot_child]] decided to try a funny cure, so [[slot_cure_action]] to the plant.
s4: Then [[slot_child]] played a soft [[slot_music_type]] with the plant.
s5: The next day, [[slot_child]] noticed the soil was dry and the sun was hidden by clouds.
s6: [[slot_child]] poured water into the pot and moved the plant to a sunny spot.
s7: The centerpiece moment: [[slot_child]] put headphones on the pot so the plant could listen to their favorite song.
s8: After the water and light, the [[slot_plant]] perked up and showed a [[slot_change]].
s9: [[slot_child]] smiled, happy that the plant was no longer sad.
s10: The window sill felt brighter, and both [[slot_child]] and the plant enjoyed the sunshine together.
