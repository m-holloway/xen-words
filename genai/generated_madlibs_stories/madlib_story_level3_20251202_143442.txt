Title: The Kite That Learned to Sing
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child, a pet, and a bright kite find a gentle way to solve a small problem and celebrate together.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): The name of the main child character.
  Child prompt: What is your favorite name for a child?
  Examples: Mia, Leo, Sam
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy or friendly animal the child might like.
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: bunny, puppy, kitten
- slot_toy (toy_object): A small toy that can be played with outdoors.
  Child prompt: Name a toy you like to play with in the park.
  Examples: kite, ball, spinning top
- slot_place (place_name): A place where the child can play.
  Child prompt: Where do you like to play?
  Examples: backyard, park, garden
- slot_color (color): A bright color for the toy.
  Child prompt: What is a bright color you like?
  Examples: red, blue, yellow
- slot_weather (weather_condition): The weather in the story.
  Child prompt: What is the weather like today?
  Examples: sunny, cloudy, windy
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack the child can share.
  Child prompt: What is a snack you like to eat after playing?
  Examples: apple, banana, candy

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_pet]] and a [[slot_toy]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: The [[slot_toy]] was bright [[slot_color]] and danced in the [[slot_weather]] sky.
s3: One day, the kite got stuck high in a tall tree.
s4: [[slot_child]] looked up and felt a little sad.
s5: [[slot_child]] thought about climbing the tree, but then remembered that [[slot_pet]] liked to hop.
s6: Instead of climbing, [[slot_child]] sang a gentle song to the kite.
s7: The kite twitched and, with a soft sigh, fell back to the ground.
s8: [[slot_child]] and [[slot_pet]] cheered and shared a [[slot_snack]].
s9: [[slot_child]] felt happy when the kite flew again.
s10: They played hop and chase with the kite for a long time.
s11: When the sun began to set, [[slot_child]] tucked the kite into a basket.
s12: [[slot_child]] said, “We’ll fly again tomorrow.”
s13: The backyard looked peaceful, and [[slot_child]] felt calm.
s14: [[slot_child]] thanked the wind for the gentle breeze.
s15: They all went home, dreaming of bright colors.
s16: The next day, the kite was ready for another adventure.
