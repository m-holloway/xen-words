Title: The Wiggling Toy and the Kind Heart
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child shares a toy that falls into water, helping it and sharing a snack, and the story ends with a gentle, happy moment.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): The name of the main character.
  Child prompt: Can you give a name for the child in the story?
  Examples: Mia, Sam, Lily
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A friendly animal the child might like.
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy that the child plays with.
  Child prompt: Can you think of a toy the child might have?
  Examples: ball, doll, robot
- slot_color (color): A bright color for the toy.
  Child prompt: Can you name a color?
  Examples: yellow, purple, green
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack the child can share.
  Child prompt: Can you name a snack you like?
  Examples: cookies, apple slices, granola bar
- slot_place (place_name): A place where the child plays.
  Child prompt: Can you name a place where you like to play?
  Examples: park, garden, backyard
- slot_sound (sound): A sound that can describe the toy's wiggle.
  Child prompt: Can you think of a sound that feels happy?
  Examples: whoosh, ding, hum

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: The toy was a [[slot_color]] [[slot_toy]] that liked to wiggle.
s3: One day, the toy slipped off a bench and fell into a puddle.
s4: [[slot_child]] felt sad, but then thought of [[slot_pet]].
s5: [[slot_child]] ran to [[slot_pet]] and whispered, "Help!"
s6: [[slot_pet]] nuzzled the toy and lifted it out of the water.
s7: The toy squeaked, and [[slot_child]] giggled.
s8: [[slot_child]] decided to share a [[slot_snack]] with [[slot_pet]].
s9: They sat on a blanket and watched the clouds.
s10: [[slot_child]] imagined the toy dancing like a [[slot_sound]].
s11: The toy's wiggle turned into a gentle rhythm.
s12: [[slot_child]] felt warm inside.
s13: When the sun set, they waved goodbye to the toy.
s14: [[slot_child]] promised to bring [[slot_toy]] to the [[slot_place]] again.
s15: The [[slot_place]] felt brighter with the toy's happy wiggle.
s16: And they all lived happily, playing and sharing.
