Title: Milo's Magic Kite
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A gentle story about Milo learning to ask for help and sharing joy with a puppy while flying a kite.

Slots:
- slot_toy (toy_object): A small toy that Milo can play with.
  Child prompt: Can you name a toy you like to play with?
  Examples: kite, balloon, toy car
- slot_color (color): A bright color for the toy.
  Child prompt: What is a bright color you like?
  Examples: blue, green, pink
- slot_place (place): A place where Milo plays.
  Child prompt: Can you name a place you like to go to?
  Examples: park, garden, meadow
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A friendly animal that Milo has.
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_sound (sound): A soft sound the kite makes.
  Child prompt: What is a soft sound you hear in nature?
  Examples: whir, hum, chirp
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack Milo can share with his pet.
  Child prompt: What is a snack you like to eat?
  Examples: cookie, apple, carrot

Story template:
s1: Milo loved to play with his [[slot_toy]] in the [[slot_place]].
s2: The [[slot_toy]] was bright [[slot_color]] and looked like a rainbow dragon.
s3: He tried to lift it up, but the wind blew it down.
s4: Milo felt a little sad.
s5: He almost put the [[slot_toy]] on the ground and say goodbye.
s6: Then he remembered his [[slot_pet]] who loved to chase butterflies.
s7: Milo whispered, "Come on, [[slot_pet]]! Let's help me."
s8: The [[slot_pet]] wagged its tail and jumped, catching the kite's string.
s9: Together, they lifted the kite higher, and it sang a soft [[slot_sound]].
s10: Milo and his [[slot_pet]] shared a [[slot_snack]] while watching the kite dance.
s11: The kite flew over the [[slot_place]] and painted the sky with colors.
s12: Milo felt happy and thanked his friend.
s13: They promised to play again tomorrow.
