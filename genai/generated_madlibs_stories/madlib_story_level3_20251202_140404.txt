Title: Mia's Missing Button Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A gentle story about Mia, her stuffed animal, and a friendly friend learning to help each other.

Slots:
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A cozy or friendly animal the child might like.
  Child prompt: Can you name an animal you would love to be friends with?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_toy (toy_object): A favorite toy that the child plays with.
  Child prompt: What is your favorite toy?
  Examples: teddy bear, plastic dinosaur, foam puzzle
- slot_friend (friend_name): A friend's name.
  Child prompt: What is your best friend's name?
  Examples: Sam, Lily, Tom
- slot_tree (tree_type): A type of tree in the park.
  Child prompt: What kind of tree do you see in the park?
  Examples: oak, maple, palm
- slot_water_feature (water_feature): A water feature in the park.
  Child prompt: What is a water feature you see in a park?
  Examples: fountain, pond, stream
- slot_sound (sound_type): A sound that sounds like a giggling bell.
  Child prompt: What is a sound that sounds like a giggling bell?
  Examples: tinkle, ring, clink
- slot_color (color): A bright color.
  Child prompt: Name a bright color you like.
  Examples: yellow, orange, pink
- slot_snack_food (snack_food): A snack food the child might share.
  Child prompt: What is a snack food you like to share?
  Examples: apple slice, cookie, granola bar

Story template:
s1: Mia, a bright little girl, loved to play with her favorite stuffed [[slot_pet]] in the sunny park.
s2: One day, her [[slot_toy]] had a missing button, and Mia felt a little sad.
s3: She looked around and saw her friend [[slot_friend]] waving from the swing set.
s4: Mia thought she could just keep playing, but then she remembered how [[slot_friend]] always helped her.
s5: She walked over and said, 'Can you help me find the button?'
s6: Together they searched under the big [[slot_tree]] and near the sparkling [[slot_water_feature]].
s7: Suddenly, they heard a tiny [[slot_sound]] that sounded like a giggling bell.
s8: They followed the sound and found the button stuck in a patch of bright [[slot_color]] moss.
s9: Mia gently pulled it out, and the toy clicked back into place.
s10: She smiled and gave the [[slot_pet]] a big hug, feeling warm like a sunrise.
s11: Then she invited [[slot_friend]] to share a [[slot_snack_food]] under the shade.
s12: They laughed as the [[slot_snack_food]] fell like tiny rain drops on the grass.
s13: Mia realized that sharing made the day brighter, just like the bright sunlight.
s14: They promised to always help each other, no matter how small the problem.
s15: When the sun set, they waved goodbye, feeling happy and safe.
s16: And they went home, dreaming of new adventures with their friends and toys.
