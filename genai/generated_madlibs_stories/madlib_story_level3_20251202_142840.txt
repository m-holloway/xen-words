Title: The Music of Kindness
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A kind child helps a lost pet find its blanket and they all share a snack.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): A name for the main child character
  Child prompt: What is your favorite name for a child?
  Examples: Mia, Liam, Sofia
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy that makes music
  Child prompt: What is a toy that makes music?
  Examples: musical doll, singing kite, talking toy
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A friendly animal
  Child prompt: What is a friendly animal you like?
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_blanket (color): Color of the blanket
  Child prompt: What is a bright color?
  Examples: red, blue, yellow
- slot_insect (insect): A helpful insect
  Child prompt: What is an insect that could help?
  Examples: ladybug, butterfly, beetle
- slot_snack (snack_food): A tasty snack
  Child prompt: What is a snack you like?
  Examples: cookies, fruit, pretzel

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the park.
s2: One bright day, [[slot_toy]] sang a soft tune that made the trees sway.
s3: As they walked, they saw a [[slot_pet]] with sad eyes.
s4: The [[slot_pet]] was looking for its favorite blanket, a bright [[slot_blanket]].
s5: [[slot_child]] felt sorry and wanted to help.
s6: First, [[slot_child]] asked a friendly [[slot_insect]] for clues.
s7: The [[slot_insect]] pointed to a hill where the blanket might be.
s8: [[slot_child]] almost ran to the hill, but remembered the [[slot_pet]]'s lonely heart.
s9: Instead, [[slot_child]] stayed and sang with [[slot_toy]], hoping to cheer the [[slot_pet]].
s10: The music grew louder, and the [[slot_blanket]] floated down like a tiny rainbow.
s11: [[slot_child]] picked up the blanket and gave it to the [[slot_pet]].
s12: The [[slot_pet]] wagged its tail, and its eyes sparkled like stars.
s13: [[slot_toy]] sang a happy note, and the park smelled of fresh daisies.
s14: Together, they shared a tasty [[slot_snack]] under the big oak tree.
s15: [[slot_child]] felt warm inside, knowing kindness makes friends.
s16: The day ended with a gentle breeze and the sound of laughter.
