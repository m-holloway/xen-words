Title: The Lost Toy Adventure
Level: 3 (Grades 1–2)
Target minutes: 5
Summary: A child, a pet, and a friendly animal find a lost toy in a magical park.

Slots:
- slot_child (child_name): Child's name
  Child prompt: What is the name of the child in the story?
  Examples: Mia, Liam, Sofia
- slot_pet (animal_pet): A friendly animal the child might have.
  Child prompt: Name a pet you would like.
  Examples: puppy, bunny, kitten
- slot_toy (toy_object): A toy the child likes to play with.
  Child prompt: What toy do you love?
  Examples: ball, doll, car
- slot_friend (child_name): A friend of the child.
  Child prompt: Name a friend of the child.
  Examples: Emma, Noah, Ava
- slot_friendly_animal (animal_pet): A kind animal that helps.
  Child prompt: Name a friendly animal that could help.
  Examples: squirrel, rabbit, deer
- slot_mysterious_object (object): A strange object in the park.
  Child prompt: Name a strange object you might see in a park.
  Examples: rainbow mushroom, glowing rock, giant leaf
- slot_color (color): Color of the toy.
  Child prompt: What color is the toy?
  Examples: pink, blue, green
- slot_snack (snack_food): A snack to share.
  Child prompt: Name a snack you like to eat.
  Examples: cookies, fruit, sandwich

Story template:
s1: [[slot_child]] loved to play with [[slot_toy]] in the park.
s2: [[slot_child]] had a [[slot_pet]] that liked to nap under the big oak tree.
s3: One sunny day, [[slot_child]] carried a bright [[slot_color]] [[slot_toy]] to the playground.
s4: When they reached the sandbox, the [[slot_toy]] was gone.
s5: [[slot_child]] looked left and right, but the toy was not there.
s6: [[slot_child]] almost ran home, but decided to keep looking.
s7: A friendly [[slot_friendly_animal]] hopped over, asking if something was missing.
s8: [[slot_child]] told the [[slot_friendly_animal]] about the missing toy.
s9: The [[slot_friendly_animal]] led them to a [[slot_mysterious_object]] that glowed like a rainbow.
s10: Inside the [[slot_mysterious_object]], the [[slot_toy]] floated like a bright [[slot_color]] balloon.
s11: [[slot_child]] thanked the [[slot_friendly_animal]] and brought the toy home.
s12: At home, [[slot_child]] shared the toy with [[slot_friend]] and they ate a [[slot_snack]] together.
s13: They laughed and played until the sun set.
s14: [[slot_child]] felt happy that the toy was safe and that friends help each other.
s15: The park looked quiet, but the trees whispered stories of the day.
s16: [[slot_child]] promised to always look for the [[slot_toy]] before leaving home.
